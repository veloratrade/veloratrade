Forex Trading Journal: How to Track and Review Your Forex Trades

Forex is the largest financial market in the world, and successful forex traders share one trait: they record and analyze every trade. Learn how to build an effective forex trading journal.

ForexTrading journalMarket Analysis
8 min read VELORA

Why Forex Needs a Dedicated Journal

The forex market operates 24 hours a day with diverse currency pairs influenced by economic, political, and geographical factors. Without a journal, identifying which pair, session, and strategy works best for you is practically impossible.

Forex traders typically execute many trades per day, making systematic recording even more critical.

Forex-Specific Journal Fields

Beyond general fields, a forex journal should include currency pair, trading session (London, New York, Tokyo), spread, interest rate, relevant economic events, and currency correlation.

Recording the trading session is crucial. Many traders discover that 70% of their profits come from just one specific session.

Performance Analysis in Forex

With a forex journal, you can compare win rate per currency pair, average R-Multiple per session, best trading hours, and performance of different strategies.

This analysis shows whether you should focus on specific pairs or change your strategy entirely.

Key Insight: Did you know? Traders who record their trading session identify profitable patterns 40% faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which currency pair is best for journaling?

Every pair you trade should be recorded. EURUSD, GBPUSD, and USDJPY typically have the highest trading volume.

Should I record losing trades too?

Yes, recording losing trades is even more important than winning trades. They show you where you are making mistakes.
