This document explains how VELORA handles data when you use its website, trading journal and analytical features. By using the services, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this policy.

01 / Scope

Our privacy commitment

VELORA is a trading journal and trader-performance analysis tool. We process only the data needed to provide, secure and reasonably improve the services. VELORA is not a financial adviser and does not use your data to make or execute trades.

02 / Collection

What data may be collected?

The data involved depends on the features you choose. For example, connecting a trading account or recording a trade provides VELORA with information related to that feature.

Account informationName, email, language settings and the identifiers needed to manage your account.
Trading dataJournal records, performance statistics, notes and data that you enter or synchronise.
Technical informationBrowser type, security logs, IP address and data needed to diagnose faults and prevent abuse.
Support communicationsRequests, feedback and correspondence you send when contacting our team.
03 / Use

How do we use data?

  • To provide and personalise journal, reporting and performance-analysis features.
  • To protect accounts, identify suspicious activity and maintain service reliability.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important service changes.
  • To improve the product using aggregated patterns that are non-attributable wherever possible.
04 / Sharing

Your data is not for sale.

We do not sell your personal data or trading-performance information, nor provide it to brokers for marketing. We may share only the minimum necessary data with service providers bound by confidentiality in the following circumstances:

  • Hosting infrastructure, email delivery and technical support needed to operate the service.
  • When required by valid law or a court order.
  • To protect the rights and safety of users, VELORA or the public against fraud and abuse.
05 / Security and retention

Protection proportionate to data sensitivity

We use established technical and organisational safeguards, including restricted access, secure data transmission and security-event monitoring. No digital transmission or storage method is absolutely guaranteed, so we recommend using a unique password and appropriate authentication.

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce agreements. We then delete or anonymise it.

06 / Your rights

You are in control.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of your personal data, obtain a copy, restrict processing or delete your account. To protect security, we may verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

07 / Updates

We communicate changes transparently.

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the product, legal requirements or security practices. For material changes, we will publish the new version on this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or in-product notice. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated version.

08 / Contact

Have a privacy question or request?

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is processed, the VELORA privacy team is ready to help. We normally review requests as quickly as possible.

Data control should be simple.

Contact the privacy team to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data.
